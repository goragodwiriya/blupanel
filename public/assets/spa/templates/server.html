What this does not do

The panel sets the name and keeps /etc/hosts in step. It cannot set the reverse DNS of your IP — do that with your cloud provider, or strict mail servers will reject mail from this machine. Certificates naming the old host stay as they are, and Postfix keeps announcing the old name until it restarts.
